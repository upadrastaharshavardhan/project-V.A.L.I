# Author and Concept Attribution

## Concept originator

**Upadrasta Harsha Vardhan**

VALI—as a named idea connecting:

- progressive deception,
- isolation from production,
- conversion of attacker effort into defender intelligence,
- and the cultural metaphor of Vāli from the *Rāmāyaṇa*,

—is presented in this documentation package as **his original conceptual contribution**.

## Documentation scope

This package describes:

1. The concept and goals (including a 2030 horizon)  
2. The architecture and technical design of a reference implementation  
3. The evolution of that implementation across versions **V1 through V10**

## Inspiration acknowledgment

Narrative inspiration is drawn from the *Rāmāyaṇa* figure **Vāli (Vali)**, used strictly as a **design metaphor** for asymmetric advantage under confrontation and for preferring indirect defense of real assets. Literary tradition is not treated as a scientific authority for software behavior.

## Defensive purpose

All technical recommendations assume **lawful defensive use** on systems the operator owns or is authorized to protect.
