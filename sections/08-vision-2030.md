# Vision to 2030

## 1. Strategic statement

By **2030**, VALI aims to mature from a strong reference deception fabric into a **widely deployable defensive layer** that organizations treat as normal infrastructure—like backups or identity SSO—while remaining faithful to the founding idea:

> Adversarial effort against protected systems should strengthen the defender’s awareness and control of the defensive engagement—not the attacker’s reach into real assets.

**Concept originator:** Upadrasta Harsha Vardhan  

---

## 2. Capability horizons

### 2026–2027 — Solidify the core

- Harden isolation patterns for production-adjacent lab use  
- Expand protocol coverage beyond web/SSH  
- Improve decoy content generation pipelines  
- Formal evaluation datasets and red-team exercises  

### 2028–2029 — Organizational intelligence

- Multi-node deception meshes  
- Stronger campaign graph analytics  
- Managed sharing formats (STIX and beyond) with governance  
- Tighter integration with SOC orchestration  

### 2030 — Standard defensive stratum

- VALI-class controls as a common architecture pattern  
- Adaptive surfaces with policy-bounded AI assistance  
- Measurable reduction in time-to-high-fidelity awareness for intrusion attempts  
- Educational presence in university security curricula as a case study in ethical deception  

---

## 3. Research agenda

1. **Detectability** — minimizing honeypot tells without unsafe realism  
2. **Causal intelligence** — from events to likely objectives  
3. **Cross-domain correlation** — identity, endpoint, and deception signals  
4. **Privacy-preserving collaboration** — sharing attacker patterns without exposing victims  
5. **Human factors** — analyst trust in progressive deception narratives  

---

## 4. Product principles that must not erode

Even in 2030:

1. Isolation over spectacle  
2. No retaliation as a default feature  
3. Explainable intelligence over opaque scores alone  
4. Operator control over autonomous aggression  
5. Honesty about limits of attribution  

---

## 5. Success picture (narrative)

An organization runs production services with ordinary controls. Beside them runs a VALI fabric. When opportunistic or targeted adversaries probe, they spend hours on progressive decoys. SOC receives canary-grade alerts, campaign narratives, and exportable indicators. Real systems remain untouched. The adversary’s playbook becomes the defender’s lesson library.

That is the 2030 expression of the Vali metaphor—**implemented as engineering, constrained by ethics**.
